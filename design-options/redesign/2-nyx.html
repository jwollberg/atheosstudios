Γ · The Grave
I

First-person zombie survival

Grave Error

You don't beat this world. You outlast it.

It began with a software update. The HACK program put adaptive nanobots in billions of minds, then Core Update 3.0 shipped with a fatal flaw and the dead got back up. You're a Null, one of the few the implants never took. The Hacks are whoever used to live here. They track you by sound and by smell, they slow down in daylight, and only a ruined brain keeps one down.

  • 3D, first-person
  • Solo or co-op
  • PC
  • Coming eventually

Ε · The Deep
II

2D dig-and-build platformer

Elderdeep

The deep is somewhere you walk to.

A cute dig-and-build game on a world that keeps going. Undercut a cliff and it comes down on you. Chop through a trunk and the tree falls over. Where water meets lava you get obsidian. Six cavern roads run underneath it all, and the towns along them are full of people who each remember what you did to them. Humans, dwarves and goblins all dig at the same speed. Only the humans have to duck.

  • 2D, endless world
  • Solo or multiplayer
  • PC
  • In development

Η · The Hold
III

Top-down survival tower defense

HoldoutWorking title

Everyone you save gets a job.

A few weeks into the outbreak, somewhere in rural America, you have a pistol, a knife and a day's food. The dead walk in from the edges of the map, and each one drops whatever it was carrying when it turned. The survivors you take in all have names, and they all need a post: up the watchtower, behind the sandbags, in the medic tent. Gunfire works. It also brings more of them.

The studio

Why it takes a while.

Atheos Studios is an independent game developer. We don't license an engine. Each game runs on code we wrote ourselves, down to how far a gunshot carries and how long a dwarf holds a grudge. It's the slow way to make games, but it means each world works the way we want it to, not the way an engine expects.
